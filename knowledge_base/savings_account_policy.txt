SAVINGS ACCOUNT POLICY

Minimum Balance: Customers must maintain a minimum average monthly balance of $500 in a Regular Savings Account. Falling below this incurs a $15 monthly maintenance fee.

Interest Rate: Regular Savings Accounts earn 2.5% annual interest, calculated daily and credited quarterly.

Eligibility: Any individual aged 18 or older with a valid government ID and proof of address can open a Savings Account. Minors aged 13-17 may open a Minor Savings Account with a parent or guardian as co-signer.

Withdrawal Limits: Customers can make up to 6 free withdrawals per month. Additional withdrawals incur a $5 fee per transaction.

Account Closure: Accounts inactive for 24 consecutive months are classified as dormant. Dormant accounts incur a $10 monthly dormancy fee until reactivated or closed.
