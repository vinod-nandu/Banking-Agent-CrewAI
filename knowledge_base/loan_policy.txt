PERSONAL LOAN POLICY

Eligibility Criteria: Applicants must be 21-60 years old, have a minimum monthly income of $2,000, and a credit score of 650 or higher.

Loan Amount: Personal loans range from $1,000 to $50,000 based on income and credit history.

Interest Rate: Personal loans carry an interest rate of 9.5% to 14.5% APR, depending on credit score and loan tenure.

Tenure: Loan repayment tenure ranges from 12 to 60 months.

Processing Fee: A one-time processing fee of 2% of the loan amount is charged upfront.

Prepayment: Customers can prepay the loan after 6 months with no prepayment penalty. Prepayment before 6 months incurs a 2% penalty on the outstanding amount.

Required Documents: Valid government ID, proof of income (last 3 months' pay stubs or tax returns), proof of address, and bank statements for the last 6 months.
